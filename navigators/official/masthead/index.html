Each packet of the Foundational Series holds twelve memory verses on six topics. Here every packet is sung straight through, word for word from the King James Version, with the reference sung after each verse so the address is learned along with the words.

Every packet has its own musical style, chosen so that the tunes stay apart in memory. Listen here, or download the recordings to keep.

How to use them

Use the repeat button on a packet to play it over and over while you learn it. The reference is sung after each verse, so say the address with the words. Once the packets are solid, use Shuffle all to keep them fresh in any order.

Locked and Proposed

Packet A is the recording already being memorized and will not change. Packets marked Proposed are the best takes made so far and may still be replaced by a better one.

Text

Every verse is sung from the King James Version, which is in the public domain. The verse selection follows the Sixty-Pack of the Foundational Series.
