How to use these

Each song walks through its packet in order: the topic, then each verse sung in full, then the reference. Listen to a packet on repeat for a week — in the car, while walking, while washing up — and let the tune carry the text until you can sing it without the recording.

  • Locked means the recording is final and being memorized as-is. Proposed means it has passed screening and is awaiting a final listen; it may still be replaced.
  • Every lyric is the King James text verbatim. Recordings are screened for spoken-word passages and word-for-word lyric fidelity before being published here.
  • Links point to the canonical file for each packet, so a bookmark keeps working if a recording is re-selected.
