## Validation Report

- **Packets found**: 5 lettered packets (A, B, C, D, E) — the Foundational
  Series 60 Pack. Additionally, the source contains 5 numbered "Series"
  (Series 1–5) that are not lettered packets but are structurally
  analogous; these are recorded in Part 2.
- **Topics per packet (A–E)**: 6 topics each, all following the 2 verses
  per topic pattern. No anomaly.
- **Topics per Series**: Series 2, 3, and 4 each have three named
  subsections of 6 paired topics apiece (2 verses/topic), consistent with
  the packet pattern. Series 1 and Series 5 deviate — see Anomalies.
- **Verses per packet**: A = 12, B = 12, C = 12, D = 12, E = 12
  (total 60 — matches the standard TMS 5 × 6 × 2 structure exactly).
- **Verses per Series**: Series 1 = 36, Series 2 = 36, Series 3 = 36,
  Series 4 = 36, Series 5 = 36 (total 180).
- **Grand total verses captured in structured tables**: 240 (60 + 180).
- **Anomalies**:
  1. The source PDF's overall content is much larger than the "standard
     TMS" 5-packet/6-topic/2-verse structure the procedure describes as
     the baseline. Beyond the 5 lettered packets (A–E, 60 verses, which do
     match the standard exactly), the PDF also contains 5 further numbered
     Series (Series 1–5, 180 verses) that are not lettered packets. These
     are recorded in Part 2 above rather than omitted.
  2. Within Series 1, the "God the Father" subsection (1-25 to 1-36) breaks
     the 2-verses-per-topic pattern used everywhere else in Series 1–4:
     each of its 12 designators has its own unique topic name (12 distinct
     topics for 12 verses), rather than being paired under 6 shared topic
     names.
  3. Series 5 ("Sharing Your Faith") breaks the 2-verses-per-topic pattern
     throughout all three of its subsections (Evangelism, Excuses,
     Believer's Position in Christ): every one of its 36 designators has
     its own unique topic name (36 distinct topics for 36 verses).
  4. Page 1 of the PDF contains an unlabeled sample/cover page titled
